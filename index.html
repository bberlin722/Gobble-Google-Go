Gobble Gobble Go!

Two teams, one phone. Act, describe or gobble your way to glory before the timer runs out.

How to play

Pass the phone to the clue-giver on the team whose turn it is. Only they look at the screen. Each word comes with a way to give the clue:

    Tap Got it! when your team guesses right, Skip if you're stuck. Most points after the last turn wins the wishbone.
